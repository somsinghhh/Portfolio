01 / THE JOURNEY

FROM FUNDAMENTALS TO SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

My trajectory in computer science started with a foundational fascination for how systems compute and coordinate under the hood. While maintaining an 8.90+ SGPA across core data structures, computational logic, and relational databases at Pillai University, I recognized that pure theory gains value only when deployed into real-world hands.

This realization propelled me into intensive hackathons, civic technology architectures like DRISHTI, and governance platforms like DARPAN. Competing under stringent time constraints forged a pragmatic builder instinct: architect cleanly, test ruthlessly, and deliver without excuses.
